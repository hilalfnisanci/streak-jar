The method

Three steps, and then nothing to learn.

The whole product is one promise, one tap and one jar. That is deliberate — a habit tool your team has to study is a habit tool your team abandons.

  1. 1

    Choose one habit

    Pick a single thing worth doing daily. One jar, one promise — and a target you can actually finish.

  2. 2

    Drop a marble

    One tap when it’s done. It takes a second, and it is the only interaction there is.

  3. 3

    Watch it fill

    The jar fills in colour. When it’s full, the team gets its moment — and the jar keeps its place on the shelf.

Why teams

Momentum you can share without ranking anyone.

Shared, not surveilled

A jar shows the team’s momentum, not an individual’s shortfall. No leaderboards, nobody ranked against anyone.

Calm by design

No notifications, no guilt mechanics, no red streak counter. Momentum you can glance at and put down.

A moment worth finishing

A full jar earns a celebration and a place on the Trophy Shelf. Finishing is the point, not an endless streak.

Build note. Dots use the marble palette from lib/jar-colors.ts (solid) with the marble shadow, so the colour system reads as the product’s, not decoration.

Trophy shelf

Full jars stay on the shelf.

Every finished jar keeps its colour and its count. Over a quarter the shelf becomes the quiet record of what your team actually kept — not a number that resets the first day someone is ill.
